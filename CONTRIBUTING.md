# Contributing to Ona

Ona's core product is closed source. We also maintain several [open-source projects](https://github.com/gitpod-io), each under the license specified in its repository. We welcome contributions in accordance with each repository's contribution guidelines. For contributions that are not trivial ("de minimis"), Ona may require contributors to sign a contributor license agreement before we can merge the contribution.

Follow the instructions for [requesting support](https://ona.com/docs/ona/troubleshooting#requesting-support).
